Anthropic Launches Claude Haiku 5.5: A Cheap, Fast Model with 1M Token Context

Anthropic has released Claude Haiku 5.5, a small but capable model featuring a 1 million token context window, priced at just $0.10 per million input tokens. Despite its size and low cost, it's positioned to handle large-context tasks efficiently, making it one of the most affordable options with such extensive context capacity on the market.

Why it matters: As an AI Engineer working with LLM applications on ServiceNow, this is directly relevant: a cheap, large-context model opens up new possibilities for cost-effective RAG pipelines, long-document processing, and agentic workflows without the usual token-cost penalty. This could be a strong candidate for production use cases where context length and cost are limiting factors.

Liquid AI Open-Sources d1-3B and d1-omni-600M: Multimodal Models That Skip Output Tokens

Liquid AI has released open-weight decision models, d1-3B and d1-omni-600M, designed for multimodal decision-making tasks without generating output tokens in the traditional sense. These models target efficient, low-latency inference scenarios where decisions (rather than generated text) are the primary output.

Why it matters: This is notable for engineers building agentic or decision-support systems where latency and inference cost matter more than generative text output. For LLM application work, exploring 'zero output token' architectures could inform more efficient designs for classification, routing, or decision layers within larger AI pipelines.
