Cloudflare's 'Precursor' Uses Behavioral Analysis to Spot Bots and AI Agents

Cloudflare has introduced Precursor, a system that continuously analyzes behavioral patterns to distinguish between human users, bots, and AI agents accessing web services. Rather than relying on static signatures, it monitors ongoing interaction patterns to make real-time classification decisions.

Why it matters: With AI agents increasingly interacting with web services and enterprise platforms, this kind of behavioral detection is directly relevant to building and securing LLM-based applications—helping you anticipate how your own AI agents might be classified or throttled by external systems, and informing design choices for agent-based tools you build.
