Australia's Migration Overhaul: Onshore Applicants Prioritized as Offshore Skilled Visas Face Stealth Cuts

Multiple reports converge on a major shift in Australia's skilled migration system. Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke has delayed a speech expected to reveal sweeping migration cuts, while related coverage indicates the skilled visa shake-up will prioritize onshore applicants (those already in Australia) over offshore candidates. The AFR describes this as a 'stealth' overhaul specifically targeting offshore skilled workers, and Prothom Alo reports that applicants currently outside Australia should expect long processing delays as a result of these policy changes.

Why it matters: As someone navigating Australia's immigration landscape from Adelaide, this directly affects your visa strategy and timeline. If you or connections are applying for skilled visas from outside Australia, these changes signal potential delays and reduced offshore quotas—making it more advantageous to secure onshore status where possible. This is a critical development to track closely given your immigration interest and location in South Australia.
