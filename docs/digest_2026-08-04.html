AI Maturity in Engineering: Where Teams Get Stuck

An InfoQ presentation outlines a five-stage framework describing how engineering organizations progress in adopting AI across the software development lifecycle (SDLC), and identifies the common bottlenecks—technical, cultural, and process-related—that cause teams to stall at each stage.

Why it matters: As an AI Engineer working toward becoming an AI Application Engineer, this framework gives you a practical lens to benchmark your own team's AI adoption maturity, anticipate common roadblocks in scaling LLM applications (e.g., ServiceNow integrations), and identify what capabilities or processes to build next to advance to higher maturity stages.

Australia's Skilled Migration Shakeup: Onshore Workers and Trades Deprioritised

The Labor government has quietly adjusted visa settings to prioritise onshore workers already in Australia over new offshore applicants. Separately, reports reveal that trade occupations ('tradies') have been ranked surprisingly low in the priority list for the skilled migration scheme, drawing criticism from industry groups. Meanwhile, overall migration numbers have fallen sharply from the post-pandemic record highs seen in recent years.

Why it matters: These policy shifts directly affect the skilled migration landscape in Australia, which is relevant to your own visa/migration considerations as a professional based in Adelaide. Falling migration intake and reprioritisation of visa categories (favouring onshore applicants, deprioritising trades) could signal tightening competition or changing criteria for skilled visa categories relevant to tech and engineering roles, making it worth monitoring for career and residency planning.
