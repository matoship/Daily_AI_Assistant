Anthropic Previews Model Hardware Standard for Physical AI Agents

Anthropic has launched a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard (MHS), a shared specification designed to let AI agents safely operate physical devices. The standard aims to create a common protocol layer between AI models and hardware, addressing safety and interoperability concerns as agentic AI moves beyond software into robotics and IoT control.

Why it matters: As an AI engineer working on LLM applications, understanding emerging standards for agent-hardware interaction could be relevant if your ServiceNow/LLM work expands into IoT or robotics integrations. 'Code-as-World' Converts Real Videos into Executable Physics Simulations

Why it matters: While more robotics-focused, this showcases cutting-edge techniques in turning unstructured data into structured, executable representations—a pattern relevant to how you might think about transforming enterprise data into actionable agent workflows.
