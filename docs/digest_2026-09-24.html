Nokia Open-Sources AnyJev: A Training-Free Layer for Calibrated LLM Decisions

Nokia has released AnyJev, an open-source, training-free layer that can be applied to any open LLM to turn it into a calibrated decision-making model—improving reliability of model outputs for classification and decision tasks without additional fine-tuning.

Why it matters: A training-free calibration layer could be valuable for improving decision reliability in enterprise LLM applications like ServiceNow workflows, potentially reducing the need for costly fine-tuning pipelines in your projects.
