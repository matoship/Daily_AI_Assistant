New Wave of Embedding Models: Perplexity's pplx-embed-v2 and Cohere's Embed 5 Target Retrieval Quality

Two major embedding model releases landed in close succession. Perplexity launched pplx-embed-v2-context-9b-preview, a contextual embedding model designed to retrieve not just answers but the supporting evidence behind them, improving transparency and groundedness in retrieval pipelines. Separately, Cohere released Embed 5, benchmarking it against Voyage 4 Large, Gemini Embedding 2, and OpenAI's embedding models, claiming competitive or superior performance across retrieval tasks.

Why it matters: As an AI Engineer working with LLM applications and ServiceNow integrations, embedding model choice directly affects RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) quality, which underpins search, chatbots, and knowledge-base features. Understanding how these new models compare on context-aware retrieval and evidence grounding can help you pick the right backbone for enterprise search or Now Assist-style features, and stay current as you grow toward an AI Application Engineer role.

AWS Strands Labs Debuts Ultra-Fast 'Decider 2B' Model for Agentic Decision-Making

AWS Strands Labs open-sourced Strands Decider 2B, a lightweight decision model that selects among options in roughly 115 milliseconds. It's aimed at agentic workflows where fast, low-latency routing or tool-selection decisions are needed without invoking a full large language model.

Why it matters: For agentic AI applications—such as orchestrating ServiceNow workflows or multi-step LLM agents—having a fast, cheap decision layer to route tasks or pick tools can significantly cut latency and cost compared to using a large model for every branching decision. This is directly relevant to building responsive agentic systems in your current and future engineering work.
