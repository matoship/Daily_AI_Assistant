Fastino's GLiNER2.5-Decide: A 340M CPU-Ready Decision Model

Fastino has released GLiNER2.5-Decide, an open-weight 340M parameter model specifically built for decision-making tasks (like classification, routing, and structured decisions) that runs efficiently on CPU rather than requiring GPU infrastructure. This makes it a lightweight alternative to large LLMs for narrow, well-defined decision tasks embedded in larger pipelines.

Why it matters: As an AI Engineer working with ServiceNow/LLM applications, small specialized models like this are highly relevant for building cost-efficient decision layers within enterprise workflows (e.g., ticket routing, approval logic) without needing expensive GPU inference—directly applicable to your work building practical LLM-powered applications on constrained infrastructure.

Perplexity's Computer Agent Learns from Its Own Mistakes via Hint-Guided Self-Distillation

Perplexity has developed a training method for its computer-use agent that leverages real failure cases combined with hint-guided self-distillation, allowing the agent to learn corrective behavior from its own past errors rather than relying solely on curated training data.

Why it matters: This self-improvement training technique is directly relevant to building more reliable LLM-based agents in enterprise settings—understanding how agents can learn from mistakes could inform how you approach reliability and error-handling in ServiceNow-integrated AI applications.
