LLMs as DSL Authors: Rethinking Domain-Specific Languages in the AI Era

This InfoQ article explores how large language models are changing the way domain-specific languages (DSLs) are designed and authored. Why it matters: As an AI Engineer building LLM applications on ServiceNow, this directly informs how you might design internal DSLs or configuration languages for workflows, letting models generate or adapt them dynamically rather than maintaining rigid schemas—useful as you move toward more autonomous AI application engineering.
