Australia Overhauls Skilled Visa Processing Order via Ministerial Direction 119

The Australian government has issued Ministerial Direction 119, changing the order in which skilled visa applications are processed. The new direction reprioritizes certain occupation streams and pathways to permanent residency, with coverage from multiple outlets explaining who gains priority under the revised system. Separately, reporting on Labor's broader migration policy notes comparisons to the Howard-era approach and highlights a delay in policy announcements after a minister missed a scheduled speech.

Why it matters: As someone based in Adelaide and likely navigating or advising on skilled visa pathways, changes to processing priority under Direction 119 could directly affect visa timelines for skilled migrants, including tech and engineering professionals. Understanding which occupations or pathways now get priority is useful for personal planning or for colleagues on skilled visas.
