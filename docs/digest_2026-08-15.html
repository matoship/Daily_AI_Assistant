Context Engineering: Why 300 Precise Tokens Outperform 100k Noisy Ones

Why it matters: As an AI Engineer building ServiceNow/LLM applications, this directly informs how you design context pipelines and prompt architecture—key to improving reliability and efficiency in the systems you build, and highly relevant to your goal of becoming an AI Application Engineer.
