Cheap LLM 'Judge' Model Routes Hard Cases to Opus for 4x Cost-Efficient Accuracy

Hightouch describes an identity-resolution pipeline where a lightweight 'Jev' model handles most judging decisions and only escalates ambiguous cases to Anthropic's Opus model. This hybrid routing approach achieves accuracy comparable to running Opus on everything, but at roughly a quarter of the cost.

Why it matters: As an AI engineer building LLM applications, this is a directly applicable pattern for your ServiceNow/LLM work: using a cheap model as a first-pass filter and reserving expensive frontier models for genuinely hard decisions. It's a concrete architecture you could prototype to cut inference costs while maintaining quality in agentic workflows.
