Tencent Open-Sources DFly2 for Faster Inference via Speculative Decoding

Tencent released DFly2, an open-source speculative decoding technique that reuses the target model's sparse KV cache to speed up inference for sparse models, aiming to reduce latency and compute costs during generation.

Why it matters: Efficient inference techniques like speculative decoding are valuable for optimizing LLM application performance and cost—useful knowledge as you work on deploying and scaling AI applications.
