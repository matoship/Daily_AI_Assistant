Anthropic Uses AI to Formalize Proof of Fermat's Last Theorem

Anthropic's research team detailed an effort to formalize Fermat's Last Theorem using AI-assisted theorem proving, demonstrating how large language models can support rigorous mathematical formalization work traditionally done by human mathematicians using proof assistants.

Why it matters: This showcases the cutting edge of what LLMs can do beyond conversational tasks—verifiable, formal reasoning—which is directly relevant to understanding the capabilities and limits of the models you work with in LLM application engineering.
