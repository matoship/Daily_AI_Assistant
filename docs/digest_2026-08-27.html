Agentic AI Coding: Will It Replace Junior Engineers?

This piece examines the conditions that would need to hold for agentic coding systems to genuinely replace junior software engineers, rather than just accelerate their work. It argues that current agentic coding tools excel at well-specified, bounded tasks but still struggle with ambiguous requirements, codebase-wide context, and judgment calls that junior engineers develop over time. The article lays out specific technical and organizational thresholds (reliability, verification, long-horizon planning) that would need to be crossed before full replacement becomes plausible.

Why it matters: As an AI Engineer working on LLM applications, this directly informs how you might design agentic workflows and where human oversight remains essential. It's also relevant to your career trajectory toward AI Application Engineer, helping you understand realistic capability boundaries versus hype when building or advocating for agentic systems at work.
