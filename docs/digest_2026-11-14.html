Google Open-Sources AQuA: An Agent for Diagnosing Production AI Agent Failures

Google has open-sourced Ambient Quality Agent (AQuA), a tool designed to automatically diagnose failures in production AI agents by passively monitoring ambient signals and behaviors, rather than relying solely on explicit logging or user-reported bugs.

Why it matters: This is highly relevant to your work building and maintaining LLM applications—observability and failure diagnosis for agentic systems is one of the hardest unsolved problems in production AI engineering. AQuA offers a potential pattern or tool you could adapt for monitoring ServiceNow-integrated AI agents, directly supporting your trajectory toward becoming a more mature AI Application Engineer.

Perplexity Releases Decider v1.3, Adding 'Locate' to Open-Weight Decision Models

Perplexity has updated its open-weight Decider model family to v1.3, introducing a new 'Locate' capability. Decider models are designed to make structured decisions, and this release expands their functional scope, likely enabling grounding or targeting of actions/objects within a given context.

Why it matters: Open-weight decision models are a useful building block for agentic pipelines where you need lightweight, controllable reasoning components rather than relying entirely on large general-purpose LLMs. The new 'Locate' feature could be applicable to building more precise, auditable automation steps within ServiceNow workflows.
