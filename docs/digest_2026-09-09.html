Reducto's r-1 Model Slashes Document Parsing Errors by 20% at Just 1 Cent Per Page

Reducto has released r-1, a single-pass document parsing model designed to extract structured data from complex documents (PDFs, forms, tables) with a 20% reduction in errors compared to prior approaches, all at a cost of roughly 1 cent per page. The model aims to streamline document ingestion pipelines that typically require multiple passes or heavy post-processing.

Why it matters: As an AI Engineer working on LLM applications, document parsing is often a critical preprocessing step for RAG pipelines and enterprise data ingestion. A cheaper, more accurate single-pass parser like r-1 could directly reduce infrastructure costs and error rates in ServiceNow or similar workflows that rely on extracting structured data from unstructured documents—relevant to your goal of building robust AI applications.
